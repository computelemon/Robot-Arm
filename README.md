# Robot-Arm
During my senior year of high school, my first project with my new 3d printer was to try and design and fabricate a robot arm. Sadly I did not advance far beyond prototyping joint mechanisms. Funding issues, developing control software, and college all got in the way.


This is the project that I first got to use CAD. I selected Fusion360 and got to teaching myself how to design a geared joint. My goal, retrospectively being stupid, was to design this arm completely on my own with no outside assistance. 

I selected a NEMA 17 motor to drive a 3 tiered planetary reduction joint as a cycloidal drive proved too challenging to manufacture.
